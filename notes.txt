Agentic AI
- refers to a system that includes multipe AI agents
- think, act and adapt
- AI agents can work together or collaborate to solve problem
- Refine its approach based on real world interaction. Eg. analyze trend, customer feedback with minimum user input
- act based on pre-defined condition 
- can break down complex goals into simpler forms; refine strategy automatically
- Eg Devin from Cognition AI
- Eg in supply chain : track order, reorder inventory and plan routes. Navigate disruption seamlessly eg. wearhouse robots navigates with no user intervetion by autonomously inspecting the settings

Perception

Analysis
- interprets collected data to determine output
- Role: Neural Network to predict future trend

Decision
- Mix of rule based (reinforcement) or probabilistic model to determine the decision

Action
- Executes necessary steps to achieve desired output


Cognitive Framework
- to reason, plan and act more effectively
- 2 most common frameworks are : 
		1. SORE (State, Operator, and Result)
			State - current situation or environment that the AI agent perceives
			Operator - possible actions or steps the agent can take to change the current state
			Result - outcome after applying an operator to the current state

		2. BDI (Belief, Desire, Intention)
			Belief - Information the agent has about the world
			Desire - goal or outcome the agent aims to achieve
			Intention - plan or course of action the agent takes in order to achieve its desired goals
